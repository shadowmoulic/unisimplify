THE DIVERSE SCHOOLS OF SAI UNIVERSITY
School of Law
School of Law
Sai University School of Law is a dynamic hub where legal tradition meets cutting-edge innovation. 
As an integral part of Sai University, a leading institution in India, we are committed to redefining the legal landscape through a forward-thinking, interdisciplinary approach. At Sai University School of Law, we prepare you to thrive in a data-driven world. Our curriculum is designed to empower you to become not just a legal expert, but a leader at the intersection of law and technology. 
We are at the forefront of integrating technology, law, and society studies. We are the first law school in India to offer dedicated tech law modules across our curriculum, as well as, specialized courses in Science, Technology, and Society (STS) to provide crucial interdisciplinary insights into how scientific advancements and technological innovations shape societal values, legal systems, and ethical considerations. 
At the heart of our approach lies a deep commitment to liberal education and interdisciplinary learning. At Sai University School of Law, you will gain invaluable insights from experts across diverse fields, learning with students from various disciplines. This rich, collaborative environment fosters a holistic understanding of law and its impact on society. Our students are afforded the unique opportunity to take courses from across the various schools at Sai University – our collaboration with the School of Artificial Intelligence, School of Computing and Data Sciences, School of Technology, School of Business, School of Media Studies, and School of Arts, and Sciences offers unparalleled opportunities for students to prepare for the legal challenges and opportunities presented by emerging trends. 
If you are looking for a vibrant community with distinguished faculty and cutting-edge research, look no further than Sai University School of Law.

School of Computing and Data Science
School of Computing and Data Science
Sai University School of Computing and Data Science is dedicated to cultivating highly skilled professionals in the fields of computing and data analytics. As part of a liberal education university offering multidisciplinary education across seven diverse disciplines, our school emphasizes the importance of integrating knowledge from various fields to foster innovation and success in organizations. In today's digital age, computer science and data science have transcended traditional boundaries, becoming essential skills sought after in numerous industries. At Sai University, students engage in a comprehensive curriculum that combines theoretical foundations with practical applications. Through hands-on experience with industry-relevant technologies, methods, and real-world data sets, our students are equipped to tackle contemporary challenges effectively. Our commitment to a multidisciplinary approach ensures that students not only master the technical aspects of computing and data science but also develop critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills that are vital in today’s rapidly evolving landscape. At Sai University, we prepare our students to be the innovators and leaders of tomorrow.

School of Media

School of Technology
School of Technology at Sai University offers its flagship programs, Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Biotechnology and Environmental Engineering and Sustainability. Building on Sai University’s commitment to a multidisciplinary liberal education, the School of Technology aims to empower students with cutting-edge technical expertise combined with a broad understanding of societal and global challenges.
